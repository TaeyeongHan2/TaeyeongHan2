## About Me

I'm an Information & Communication Engineering student focused on **XR, real-time graphics, and virtual entertainment**.

My main development experience comes from building XR applications in Unity, including a **virtual concert platform for Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest 3**.

Through the project, I worked on **hand tracking, gesture-based interaction, voice interaction, real-time networking, and device-specific XR implementation**.

I'm currently expanding my focus toward **Unreal Engine and real-time graphics**, especially rendering, shaders, lighting, VFX, and virtual stage production.

My long-term interest is in creating interactive technology for **virtual idols, virtual concerts, immersive performances, and entertainment experiences**.

## Featured Projects

### VStage — XR Virtual Concert Platform

> An XR virtual concert platform that connects performers and audiences through real-time interaction.

🏆 **2025 K-Digital Challenge Virtual Convergence Service Developer Competition — Excellence Award**

VStage started as an Apple Vision Pro R&D project and later expanded to Meta Quest 3 and PC VR environments.

The project consists of two main clients:

### Audience Client

**Meta Quest 3 / Apple Vision Pro based XR audience experience**

- Hand tracking & gesture interaction
- Microphone recording and WAV processing
- Voice-based audience participation
- Photon Fusion multiplayer synchronization
- Host-based AI response relay
- Interactive light stick
- Recording feedback VFX
- Vision Pro → Quest 3 platform transition R&D

[View VStage Audience →](https://github.com/Virtual-Idol-Concert-Platform/VStage_Audience)

### Artist Client

**PC VR based virtual performance / host system**

- Full-body avatar tracking
- VRIK based character movement
- Facial tracking
- Networked avatar synchronization
- Performance timeline synchronization
- Host-authoritative concert control

[View VStage Artist →](https://github.com/Virtual-Idol-Concert-Platform/VStage_Artist)
